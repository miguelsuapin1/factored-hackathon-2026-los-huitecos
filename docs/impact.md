# Impact: agent time and cost

What the Dispute Desk could save, and what it costs to run. Numbers: [reports/impact_estimate.md](../reports/impact_estimate.md), generated by `analysis/impact_estimate.py` from the organizer's contact data and the evaluation of the deployed app. **It is a projection, not a measurement**: nothing here was observed in production, as the brief requires offline results to be labeled.

## The answer in one paragraph

Money disputes take about **1,900 agent-hours a year** in this bank's contact centre (9.3% of all agent time). If those disputes come through chat, the system would take **816 to 1,495 of those hours** off agents each year (4.0–7.3% of all agent time), for about **$70–130 a year** in model cost. At an illustrative $10 per loaded agent hour that is $8,000–15,000 a year for a bank of this size (150,000 customers), and it scales linearly with volume. With today's channels, where 85% of complaints arrive by phone, the immediate saving is about a tenth of that: the business case is containment *plus* moving disputes to chat.

## How each number is built

| Step | Source | Value |
|---|---|---|
| Agent time per year | `call_center_interactions`, 3 years; missing durations (14%) filled with their reason's average | 20,406 h |
| Agent time on complaints | same, `reason_category = 'Queja'` | 4,704 h |
| Share of complaints that dispute money | `complaints`, categories Transactions + Fees ([contact-reason-analysis.md](contact-reason-analysis.md)) | 40.4% |
| Agent time on disputes | complaints × dispute share | 1,902 h |
| Share the system handles without a person | [evaluation on the deployed app](../reports/eval_production-cohere.md): resolved itself (conservative) / containment | 42.9% / 78.6% |
| Agent time taken off agents | disputes × share handled | 816 – 1,495 h |
| Model cost | evaluation: $0.0044 per attempted case, $0.0082 per resolution, × 15,756 dispute contacts | $69 – $129 |

## What the estimate leaves out, on purpose

- **Dollars.** The dataset has no wage or cost data. The report gives a table at $5–20 per loaded agent hour, and `--cost-per-hour` adds a line for a real figure. Any dollar number is that assumption times the hours.
- **Faster hand-offs.** A hand-off reaches the agent with verified facts and a briefing, so the agent doesn't collect the details again. Not measured, so not counted.
- **Customer time.** A pending, reversed or declined charge is explained in one conversation instead of joining a complaint queue with a median resolution of 15–16 days. Real for those cases, but the share of disputes that are explain-only in live traffic is unknown.

## Why it could be wrong

- The two tables aren't linked (data issue C2), so the dispute share of complaint *cases* stands in for their share of complaint *calls*.
- The evaluation's conversation mix was designed to cover every rule, not to match real traffic; the real share handled without a person may be higher or lower.
- A contained conversation is counted as saving its whole handle time; some customers may still call.
- Channel shift is a business decision outside the system: the larger figure assumes it happens.
