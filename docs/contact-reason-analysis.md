# Contact-reason analysis and workflow choice

Challenge requirement #1: *"Analyze contact reasons, demand patterns, data quality, and operational constraints. Use this evidence to prioritize the workflow."*
All numbers come from [reports/contact_reasons.md](../reports/contact_reasons.md) (generated by `analysis/contact_reasons.py`) on the raw data, 686,296 contacts and 67,095 complaints from 2023-06-17 to 2026-06-18. Data caveats are in [data-issues.md](data-issues.md).

## 1. Why customers contact the bank

| Reason (raw label) | Share of contacts | First-contact resolution | Needs follow-up | Median handle time | Share of agent hours | Negative sentiment | CSAT (1–5) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Transaccional** (transactions & payments) | **35.0%** | 91.5% | 22.1% | 3.4 min | **24.0%** | 0.0% | 2.91 |
| Producto (product info) | 22.0% | 89.6% | 23.8% | 4.4 min | 18.2% | 19.2% | 2.90 |
| **Queja** (complaints) | 17.1% | **43.6%** | **63.0%** | 7.2 min | **23.1%** | 34.9% | **2.43** |
| Técnico (technical) | 15.0% | 69.9% | 40.6% | 6.0 min | 16.8% | 35.0% | 2.70 |
| Comercial (sales) | 8.0% | 65.2% | 44.5% | 9.0 min | 13.4% | 34.9% | 2.66 |
| Retención (retention) | 3.0% | 60.2% | 49.1% | 8.0 min | 4.5% | 34.9% | 2.61 |

- **Transactional contacts are the volume problem**: a third of all contacts and a quarter of agent hours. They're short and already resolved 91.5% of the time, which makes them a **cost** opportunity, not a quality one.
- **Complaints are the quality problem**: 17% of contacts but 23% of agent hours, the worst first-contact resolution (43.6%), and the lowest CSAT. Nearly two thirds need a follow-up.
- About 85% of contacts arrive by phone for every reason. Chat, WhatsApp and app are each around 3%, so a digital-first assistant has a lot of room to take phone volume.
- Demand is flat month to month. Tue–Fri carry ~16.6% each; Sunday ~8.4%.

## 2. What the complaints are about (PQR system)

| Subcategory | Cases | Share |
|---|---|---|
| **Cargo no reconocido** (unrecognized charge) | 12,297 (+1,283 in Transactions with no subcategory) | 20.2% |
| **Cobro indebido** (wrongful fee/charge) | 12,194 (+1,359 in Fees with no subcategory) | 20.2% |
| Problema con app | 12,128 (+1,279) | 20.0% |
| Atención en sucursal | 11,892 (+1,469) | 19.9% |
| Calidad de servicio | 11,886 (+1,308) | 19.7% |

**40% of all complaints (27,133 cases) dispute money**: a charge the customer doesn't recognize or a fee they consider wrong. The backlog is large for every category: ~75% of cases are still Open/In Process/Escalated, 20% breached SLA, and the median resolution is 15–16 days.

Transactions give the context disputes are checked against. Of 4.43M transactions, 5.0% were declined, 2.0% are pending and 1.0% were reversed. Many "unrecognized charge" questions in practice are pending or reversed charges, which can be answered from the data without opening a case.

## 3. Operational constraints the data imposes

1. **No natural language in the data** (transcripts, descriptions and comments are templates; [B1–B3](data-issues.md#b-text-data-is-templated-it-cant-train-or-evaluate-language-understanding)). Language understanding must be trained and evaluated on a **team-generated ES/PT utterance set**. There's also no Portuguese at all.
2. **Complaints aren't linked to transactions or to the originating contact** ([C2, C4](data-issues.md#c-broken-relationships-privacy-and-grounding)). Historical disputes can't be replayed end to end, and the service itself has to identify the transaction.
3. **`complaints.affected_product_id` points to another customer's product in 100% of cases** ([C1](data-issues.md#c-broken-relationships-privacy-and-grounding)). This is a built-in access-control trap: the service must scope every lookup to the authenticated customer.
4. **Mexican accounts are denominated in USD** ([A5](data-issues.md#a-organizer-claims-that-dont-hold)). Amounts are always shown with the record's own currency.
5. Outcomes don't differ by country, segment or accent. Fairness has to be measured on our own evaluation set.

## 4. Workflow choice (D-002, accepted by the team 2026-09-28)

### Chosen: **Transaction-dispute intake** (unrecognized charges and wrongful fees)
- **The largest quality gap**: the complaint bucket has the worst first-contact resolution (43.6%), the most follow-ups (63%), the lowest CSAT (2.43) and 23% of agent hours. Disputes are 40% of complaints.
- **It exercises every behavior the judges score**:
  - *Normal path:* the charge is found and turns out to be pending or already reversed. The system explains this from the record, and no case is needed.
  - *Ambiguous:* "I have a charge of 500 I don't recognize" matches several transactions or none, so the system asks a clarifying question.
  - *Not supported / don't act:* a request to "reverse it now" is refused. Moving money is out of scope, so the system opens a case instead.
  - *Human-required:* high fraud score, amount above a threshold, foreign merchant, repeat complainer, or regulator mention. The system hands off with a structured case: verified transaction, facts, actions taken, open questions.
- **Grounding exists**: transactions (amount, currency, merchant, date, status, response code, fraud score), products and customers.
- **Candidate learned components**: (a) an ES/PT dispute-reason/triage classifier on our labeled utterance set versus a keyword baseline; (b) a transaction matcher that ranks candidate charges from a vague description versus exact-match rules. Both can be measured on held-out data.

### Alternative: **Account & payment inquiries**
The biggest volume (35% of contacts), simple grounding, and a strong cost-per-contact story. But baseline quality is already high (91.5% first-contact resolution, 0% negative sentiment), so showing *quality* improvement is harder, and the human-handoff path is thinner.

### Not chosen
- **Card support**: there's no card-specific contact reason. Card operations (block/unblock) are only inferable from `product_status`.
- **Credit eligibility**: there's no application data, and eligibility rules would be entirely synthetic. The brief also requires a separate risk model and policy service, which is heavy for 10 days.

**Honest caveat:** the data can't isolate disputes inside contact-center traffic. "Queja" is broader than disputes, and complaint subcategories have identical outcome metrics. The case rests on volume share and on disputes being the complaint type the data can ground and verify.
