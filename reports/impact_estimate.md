## Assumptions

1. Disputes take the same share of complaint *contacts and agent time* (40.4%) as they do of complaint
   *cases* in the PQR system. The two tables aren't linked (data issue C2), so this is a proxy.
2. The evaluation's mix of conversations stands in for real dispute traffic: 42.9% resolved by the system
   (conservative) to 78.6% ended without a person. The real mix is unknown.
3. A contained conversation saves the whole agent handle time of that contact (follow-ups not counted separately).
4. Agent time is counted only where the assistant can answer: disputes moving to chat, or today's 10.5%
   of complaint contacts already in App, Web Chat or WhatsApp.
5. Missing call durations (13.9% of complaint contacts) are filled with their reason's average.
